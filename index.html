Core Capabilities

Intelligence at every
stage of the journey

Four deeply integrated pillars that transform how you discover, evaluate, and own property.

Personalized Recommendations

Our AI builds a living profile of your preferences, lifestyle, commute patterns, and financial goals — then surfaces listings ranked by how well they actually fit your life, not just your search terms.

  • Match scores based on 200+ lifestyle signals
  • Daily curated shortlist of 5–10 properties
  • AI learns and improves with every interaction
  • Neighborhood fit scores for schools, transit, safety

Market Insights

Real-time pricing intelligence powered by live transaction data, permit filings, and macro indicators. Know where the market is going before you make your move.

  • Neighborhood price trend forecasting
  • Supply/demand heat maps and velocity scores
  • Fair value estimates vs. listing price
  • Alert system for price drops & new inventory

Investment Analysis

Built for investors who need hard numbers. Every listing surfaces cap rate, projected yield, 5-year appreciation forecast, and a proprietary risk score — before you book a showing.

  • ROI projections & IRR calculations
  • Rental yield estimates by bedroom count
  • Risk scoring: vacancy rate, macro exposure
  • Comparable market analysis (CMA) on demand

End-to-End Transactions

From your first offer to closing day, Aurevia coordinates every step. Offer management, attorney matching, e-signing, and progress tracking — all in one intelligent workspace.

  • Guided offer builder with AI negotiation hints
  • Secure e-signature and document vault
  • Escrow tracking and milestone alerts
  • Curated attorney & inspector marketplace
